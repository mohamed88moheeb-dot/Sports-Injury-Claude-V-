# Hamstring strain: rebuilt protocol card (v0.1, harvest 2026-09-26)

The card is gated by criteria, not by the calendar. Fact IDs refer to `facts.json`.

## Intake (changes the track)
- **Mechanism:** sprint type or stretch/kick type. A stretch-type injury gets about a 2x longer timeline (hs-008).
- **Tendon involvement or MRI grade:** BAMIC 'c', or suspected tendon, goes to the long track with extra gates. An unknown grade with severe signs prompts a suggestion to get imaging (hs-007).
- **Red flags:** a "pop" with bruising, a palpable gap, or weakness in the sitting posture (possible proximal avulsion) means referral to a doctor.

## Pain rules (hs-004, hs-010)
- Strength work: pain up to 4/10 is fine if it has settled by the next morning.
- Running and sprinting: must be pain-free.

## Stages

| Stage | Main work | Dose | Gate to next |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 Protect/activate (days 0–5) | Isometrics at 90/90, then longer lengths; walking; L-protocol **Extender** | Extender 3×12, twice a day | Normal walking; isometric at 90/90 ≥ 70% of the other side; pain ≤ 2 walking |
| 2 Lengthen | Extender + **Diver** + **Glider**; bridges; trunk and agility (PATS) (hs-016) | Diver 3×6 every other day; Glider 3×4 every 3rd day (hs-002) | Askling H-test discomfort mostly gone; ASLR ≥ 80% of the other side; jog at 50% pain-free |
| 3 Strength at length + running | Long-length eccentrics (RDL, slider curls, **partial-range Nordics**); run at 50→70% | 2–3 strength days a week | Knee-flexor strength at ~20–30° knee angle ≥ 80% (hs-006); 3 reps at 70% pain-free |
| 4 Speed | Full Nordics; fast eccentrics / RFD; run at 80→90%; accelerations | Running ladder (hs-014) | ≥ 90% strength symmetry; pain-free 90% runs |
| 5 Sport | Sprint at ≥ 95% max velocity; sport drills; full training | Build volume toward match demands (hs-015) | Delphi RTS checklist (hs-011) + negative H-test (hs-009) + readiness score |

## RTS checklist (all required)
No pain on palpation, strength testing, flexibility testing or sprinting · knee-flexor strength ≥ 90% of the other side at both 90° and a long length · ASLR/AKE similar to the other side · negative H-test · has hit ≥ 95% max velocity · completed full training · feels ready (psychological readiness).

## What this fixes in the current plan
Sessions now change with the stage. Nordics come in partial-range at stage 3 and full-range at stage 4. The Askling L-protocol is included. Sprinting goes to ≥ 95%. Every stage has a gate. Tests (H-test, strength symmetry) are gates, not exercises.
